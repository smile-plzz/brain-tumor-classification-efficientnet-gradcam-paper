## 2. Research Focus & Objectives
1. Revisit dense-layer-augmented EfficientNetB0 architecture for four-class brain tumor classification (glioma, meningioma, pituitary, no tumor).
2. Pair predictions with Grad-CAM activation mapping for clinical interpretability.
3. Validate performance on resource-constrained consumer GPUs (GTX 1050, 8 GB).
